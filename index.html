01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

A school event.
A reason to stop by.

At a busy school event, a well-presented snack stall can be convenient for visitors, manageable for student organisers and useful for a good cause.

THE PROBLEM / 01

Visitors want a quick bite.

A simple event stall gives students and other permitted attendees a convenient place to pick up a snack.

THE SOLUTION / 02

Simple in-person sales.

Customers choose from the displayed menu and buy directly at the stall during the event, using school-approved payment methods.

THE DIFFERENCE / 03

Buy with purpose.

Rather than keeping profits for ourselves, we plan to donate all net profits to a food-relief charity.

02 / THE BUSINESS MODEL

One event.
One organised stall.

We plan to sell only during the school event, on school premises. Here is how we would run a straightforward, supervised stall.

01

Set up the stall

Prepare a compact selection of pre-packaged snacks, display prices and check stock before the event starts.

02

Sell at the event

Students serve permitted event visitors in person and take payment through the method approved by the school.

03

Count and donate

After the event, we record all sales and expenses, calculate the final net profit and arrange the approved charity donation.

We already have a separate Snack Hub website as an example of our planning. The proposed event will use an in-person stall, not everyday online orders or deliveries. View the existing site ↗

OUR PLEDGE
100% of net profits will be donated to food relief.

Net profit means sales collected at the event minus all event-related costs, including purchased stock (whether sold or unsold), supplies, giveaways and approved expenses. Any positive amount remaining is intended for Second Harvest Japan, subject to school approval.

A credible causeSecond Harvest Japan is a certified nonprofit and received Japan's Consumer Affairs Agency food-bank certification in July 2026.
Transparent follow-throughWe propose tracking sales, costs and transfers, with donation receipts available for PTA review.
07 / RESPONSIBILITY & QUESTIONS

Business-minded.
School-first.

The event stall only works if visitors, families and staff can trust how it is run. These are the safeguards we would follow with approval.

01
School supervisionOperate only at the approved school event, at the assigned stall, under school/PTA supervision and payment rules.
02
Food safety & allergiesKeep products labelled, store them appropriately and follow the school's allergy and food-handling policies.
03
Simple financial recordsRecord event stock purchases, all on-the-day sales, unsold items, other costs and the final donation, with receipts.
04
Start small. Improve fast.Buy a cautious amount for one event, track what sells and account for leftovers rather than assuming repeat sales.
Are you asking for a loan or an investment?

We are requesting up to ¥20,000 of event funding or sponsorship, not equity investment or a loan. Backers are supporting a student project and its charitable goal, not expecting a financial return.

Does all the sales money go to charity?

No. The event revenue must first cover all stock and event expenses, including unsold items and giveaways. We pledge 100% of any positive net profit after those costs.

Has the charity agreed to partner with Snack Hub?

Not yet. Second Harvest Japan is our proposed recipient. We would contact the organisation and follow the school's approved donation procedure before making any public partnership claim.

Will Snack Hub sell outside the event?

No. This proposal covers one approved school event, on school premises. It does not involve sales outside school, everyday school-day deliveries or a permanent shop.

What happens if snacks don't sell?

We would limit the opening stock purchase and account for any leftovers as event expenses. The proposed ¥3,000 reserve may help with unexpected costs, but cannot guarantee a profit.

What has already been built?

Our separate Snack Hub website contains a menu and ordering functionality, but this proposal is for in-person sales at one school event only. This pitch website does not change or replace that site.
